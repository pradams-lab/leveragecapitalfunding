# Leverage Capital Funding

An institutional-grade, high-trust, and results-oriented corporate finance platform tailored specifically for the Brazilian Merchant Cash Advance (MCA) market. Designed to bridge the gap between business owners and rapid liquidity with absolute transparency and zero banking friction.

---

## **Overview**

Leverage Capital Funding provides alternative working capital solutions and receivables-based financing (MCA) for Portuguese-speaking business owners. This repository contains the complete frontend architecture, layout systems, and conversion-optimized components designed to replace traditional, clunky financial portals with a modern, secure, and lightning-fast digital experience.

---

## **Key Features**

* **Institutional Aesthetic:** Built on a deep charcoal and navy foundation, accented with gold visual indicators and crisp typography to project absolute financial security.
* **Dual-Language Support:** Integrated native language toggle seamlessly switching between **English (EN)** and **Portuguese (PT-BR)** for regional compliance and user trust.
* **Interactive Funding Calculator:** Dynamic sliders allowing business owners to simulate requested advance amounts and flexible repayment terms in real time.
* **Verified Trust Architecture:** Embedded trust badges including BBB accreditation seals, Google 5-star review indicators, and direct click-to-call routing (`+1 (774) 205-5547`).
* **High-Converting Application Flow:** Minimalist, frictionless lead capture forms backed by end-to-end encryption notes and structured for high conversion rates.

---

## **Project Architecture & Sections**

1. **Sticky Navigation Bar:** Houses the institutional gold logo, core routing links, language switcher (EN / PT-BR), and the primary conversion CTA (*"Solicitar Capital"*).
2. **Hero Section:** High-impact value proposition highlighting 24-hour funding capability, direct phone line, and instant trust validation.
3. **Social Proof Ribbon:** Animated performance metrics and compliance tickers.
4. **About Us ("No BS") Section:** Clear, direct messaging explicitly welcoming Portuguese-speaking entrepreneurs.
5. **How It Works (3-Step Process):** Polished card grid breaking down submission, algorithmic/human risk analysis, and fast disbursement.
6. **Interactive Calculator:** Custom input sliders to estimate capital liquidity and terms.
7. **Testimonials & Case Studies:** Verified social proof from business leaders who secured rapid liquidity.
8. **Secure Application Form:** Streamlined intake inputs capturing company name, revenue metrics, and contact details.
9. **Footer:** Comprehensive regulatory disclaimers, support phone routing, and trust credentials.

* **Framework:** React / Next.js / Framer Architecture
